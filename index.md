## FAQ

### When is spring break 2027?

Most U.S. college spring breaks in 2027 fall between March 6 and March 28. Easter is March 28, 2027, so the season is early and compressed. Texas schools usually share one mid-March week.

### What are the most popular spring break destinations?

Based on our Spring Break 2026 popularity rating, the top destinations are Cancun (97/100), Punta Cana (90/100), Nassau / Bahamas (83/100), South Padre Island (80/100), Cabo San Lucas (80/100).

### When is Ultra Music Festival 2027?

Ultra Music Festival Miami runs March 26–28, 2027 at Bayfront Park, closing out Miami Music Week (March 22–28). Calvin Harris leads the Phase 1 lineup.

### Which tour companies offer Spring Break 2027 trips?

STS Travel (Cancun, Cabo, Punta Cana, Nassau, Montego Bay, Riviera Maya, Las Vegas and new Guanacaste, Costa Rica), StudentCity (Cancun, Punta Cana, Cabo, Nassau) and Inertia Tours (South Padre Island, Cancun, Cabo).

### What's new for Spring Break 2027?

STS Travel added Guanacaste, Costa Rica as an adventure-focused destination, and named Cancun, Cabo, Punta Cana and Nassau its top 2027 picks.
