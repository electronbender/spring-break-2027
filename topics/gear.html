Spring Break 2027 guide

🕶️ Packing & Gear

Pack light, pack smart. Most spring break trips need one carry-on and a beach bag.

Updated November 11, 2026

✦ Get the latest in Claude
Bag
1 carry-on + personal item
Must-have
Reef-safe sunscreen
Don't forget
Passport / REAL ID

Documents and money

  • Passport book (international) or REAL ID (domestic flights).
  • Copies of your passport, booking confirmations and insurance.
  • A debit card with no foreign transaction fees, plus some cash in small bills.

Beach and pool

  • Reef-safe, mineral sunscreen. Some Mexican eco-parks and marine areas require biodegradable sunscreen.
  • Two or three swimsuits, a cover-up, sunglasses and a hat.
  • A refillable water bottle, aloe and a waterproof phone pouch.
  • Sandals plus one pair of closed-toe shoes for clubs that require them.

Tech and extras

  • A portable charger, plus a universal adapter if needed (Mexico and most of the Caribbean use U.S. plugs).
  • Wristbands and lanyards from party packages, so don't lose them.
  • Budget airlines charge for carry-ons, so check bag rules before you pack.
