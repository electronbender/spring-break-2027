# Spring Break Cruises: Spring Break 2027 Guide

> A 3–5 night cruise to the Bahamas or Mexico bundles transport, room, food and entertainment, and lets you visit two or three ports in one week.

URL: https://springbreak.com/topics/cruises.html
Updated: 2026-11-11

## Key facts

- Typical length: 3–5 nights
- Popular ports: Nassau, Cozumel, private islands
- Drinking age: 21 on U.S. sailings

## Where they sail from

- Miami and Ft. Lauderdale: the most short Bahamas sailings, often calling at Nassau and a cruise-line private island.
- Port Canaveral and Tampa: Bahamas and western Caribbean itineraries.
- Galveston: western Caribbean and Mexico itineraries, often within driving distance for Texas students.

## Rules for college travelers

- Most major lines require at least one guest in each cabin to be 21 or older. Check your line's age policy before booking.
- The drinking age on sailings from U.S. ports is 21.
- Closed-loop cruises allow a birth certificate and photo ID, but a passport lets you fly home if you miss the ship.

## Booking tips

- Prices rise as sailings fill. Spring break weeks often sell out early.
- Drink packages, Wi-Fi, gratuities and excursions are extra.
- Get to the port the day before so a flight delay can't make you miss the ship.
