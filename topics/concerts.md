# Concerts & Festivals: Spring Break 2027 Guide

> Miami owns the last week of March: Miami Music Week runs Mar 22–28 and Ultra closes it out Mar 26–28 at Bayfront Park. Beach-town concert lineups are usually announced in December and January.

URL: https://springbreak.com/topics/concerts.html
Updated: 2026-11-11

## Key facts

- Ultra Miami: Mar 26–28 · Bayfront Park
- Miami Music Week: Mar 22–28
- Winter Music Conference: Mar 24–26

## Ultra Music Festival 2027

The 27th edition has announced its Phase 1 lineup, led by Calvin Harris. Other names reported so far include Armin van Buuren, Dom Dolla, Martin Garrix, Marshmello, Mau P, Indira Paganotto, Boys Noize and Liquid Stranger. Sofi Tukker's Animal Talk and Adam Beyer's Drumcode are hosting stage takeovers. More phases are expected before March.

## Where else to see shows

- South Padre Island: Clayton's Beach Bar hosts the island's biggest spring break concerts. Past headliners include Ice Spice (2025).
- Panama City Beach: concerts at beachfront clubs. Gulf Coast Jam (June 3–6) comes after spring break season.
- Cancun: Coco Bongo's nightly shows, plus day parties at Mandala Beach and other clubs, often included in party packages.
- Las Vegas: Dayclub and nightclub residencies at Drai's, Marquee and Wet Republic (21+).

## Ticket tips

- Buy festival passes from the official site or authorized resellers only.
- Party-package wristbands from tour companies often include cover at partner clubs. Check which nights are included.
- Most U.S. venues are 21+ for entry or drinking. Check before you buy.
