# U.S. Price Guide: Spring Break 2027 Guide

> Driving to a U.S. beach is still the cheapest spring break. What you pay depends on when you go and how many people share a room.

URL: https://springbreak.com/topics/prices-us.html
Updated: 2026-11-11

## Key facts

- Cheapest week: Week of Mar 6
- Biggest saver: Condo split 4–6 ways
- Biggest cost: Peak-week lodging

## Where the money goes

- Lodging is usually 40–60% of the trip. Prices jump during each destination's peak week.
- Party passes and concert tickets: South Padre and Panama City Beach clubs sell multi-day passes.
- Food and drinks: groceries for a condo kitchen cut costs a lot.
- Transportation: gas and parking for drive-in beaches, or airfare plus rideshares for Miami and Las Vegas.

## Destination snapshot

- South Padre Island and Panama City Beach: condo-heavy and drivable for large parts of Texas and the Southeast, so usually the best value.
- Miami and Ft. Lauderdale: pricier hotels, and Miami Music Week and Ultra (Mar 22–28) push rates higher.
- Las Vegas: midweek rooms can be cheap, but club and show costs add up. Most nightlife is 21+.
- Daytona and Lake Havasu: lower-key and budget-friendly. Havasu is built around boat rentals.

## Saving money

- Book early in the season. Cancellable rates protect you if plans change.
- Travel a week before or after your destination's peak week.
