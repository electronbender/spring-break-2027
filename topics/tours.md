# Tour Company Comparison: Spring Break 2027 Guide

> Tour companies handle flights, hotels and the party schedule for you. Here's how the big three compare for 2027.

URL: https://electronbender.github.io/spring-break-2027/topics/tours.html
Updated: 2026-11-11

## Key facts

- STS Travel: Most 2027 destinations announced
- StudentCity: Biggest party-package brand
- Inertia Tours: South Padre specialist

## STS Travel

- 2027 destinations: Cancun, Cabo, Punta Cana, Nassau, Montego Bay, Riviera Maya, Las Vegas (hotel only) and new Guanacaste, Costa Rica.
- Nassau trips start in early March at Breezes Resort & Spa.
- Optional VIP Party Package. Phone: 800-648-4849.

## StudentCity

- Destinations: Cancun, Punta Cana, Cabo and Nassau.
- Party Package: headline events, booze cruises and club nights at venues like The City, Palazzo and Mandala.
- Past headliners include Cardi B, Tiësto, Big Sean and Steve Aoki. Phone: 1-888-777-4642.

## Inertia Tours

- Based in Austin and on South Padre Island, it's the go-to operator for SPI.
- Also offers three tiers of Cancun all-inclusive resorts, and Cabo (Corazón Cabo, RIU Santa Fe).
- Phone: 800-821-2176.

## How to compare

- Compare out-the-door prices, including taxes, transfers and the party package.
- Read the deposit, payment plan and cancellation terms.
- Read recent reviews, and ask friends who traveled in 2026.
