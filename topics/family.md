# Family Spring Break: Spring Break 2027 Guide

> Family spring break peaks around the same weeks as college break. These spots skip the party scene.

URL: https://electronbender.github.io/spring-break-2027/topics/family.html
Updated: 2026-11-11

## Key facts

- #1 overall (Allianz 2026): Orlando
- Most-searched domestic: Key West (17 states)
- Trending (Google Flights): Hilo, Asheville, Sarasota

## Where families went in 2026

- Orlando was the #1 domestic spring break destination in Allianz's analysis of 1.3 million itineraries.
- Key West was the top-searched domestic spring break spot in 17 states, and Destin led in 15 states.
- Google Flights' biggest year-over-year search jumps were Hilo (Hawaii), Asheville (NC) and Sarasota (FL).

## Calmer beach picks

- Siesta Key and Sarasota: soft white sand and a calm Gulf.
- Clearwater Beach: Pier 60's nightly sunset celebration and easy walking.
- Destin and the 30A coast: emerald water and lots of condos.
- Riviera Maya: resort-based and quieter than Cancun's Hotel Zone.

## Family travel tips

- Avoid the college peak week at destinations like South Padre and Panama City Beach.
- Look for kids-stay-free and kids-eat-free resort deals.
- Children need their own passport for international flights.
