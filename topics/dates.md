# Spring Break 2027 Dates: Spring Break 2027 Guide

> Easter falls on March 28 in 2027, so the season is compressed: most college breaks land between March 6 and March 28.

URL: https://springbreak.com/topics/dates.html
Updated: 2026-11-11

## Key facts

- Peak window: Mar 6–28, 2027
- Easter Sunday: Mar 28, 2027
- Ultra Miami: Mar 26–28, 2027

## How the 2027 calendar shapes up

Most schools schedule spring break in one of four March weeks. Because Easter is early in 2027, schools that tie break to Easter week will travel at the very end of March, the same weekend as Ultra in Miami.

Texas schools usually share one week (South Padre Island called it "Spring Break Texas Week" in 2026, Mar 14–22). Expect the island's busiest week of 2027 to fall around the same mid-March dates.

## Week by week

- Week of Mar 6: early-break schools, plus quieter beaches and better hotel rates.
- Week of Mar 13: Texas and many Southern and Midwestern schools. South Padre and Panama City Beach peak.
- Week of Mar 20: Big Ten, many Northeastern schools and Miami Music Week (Mar 22–28).
- Week of Mar 27: Easter-week schools and Ultra weekend (Mar 26–28).

## Planning tips

- Confirm your school's official academic calendar before you put down a deposit.
- Fly midweek. Saturday and Sunday departures are the most expensive in peak weeks.
- If your group's breaks don't line up, the week of Mar 20 usually catches the most schools.
