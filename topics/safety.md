# Safety & Local Rules: Spring Break 2027 Guide

> Spring break 2026 showed both sides: Miami Beach was calmer than in years, while Daytona Beach logged 75 arrests in one weekend. Know the local rules before you go.

URL: https://springbreak.com/topics/safety.html
Updated: 2026-11-11

## Key facts

- U.S. drinking age: 21
- Mexico / Caribbean: 18
- Beach flags: Red = high hazard

## Local rules to expect

- Miami Beach: in 2026, arrests fell about 24% in the South Beach district. The city still uses parking limits, bag checks and a curfew it can impose if needed.
- Ft. Lauderdale and Daytona Beach: heavier police presence and curfews for under-18s in 2026.
- Many Florida beaches ban alcohol, glass containers and tents during spring break.
- City rules for 2027 are usually announced in February. Check the official city websites.

## Beach and water safety

- Florida beach flags: green means low hazard, yellow medium, red high, double red closes the water, and purple warns of dangerous marine life.
- If a rip current pulls you out, swim parallel to shore until you're free, then swim back in.
- Swim near a lifeguard tower and never swim alone or impaired.

## Personal safety

- Use the buddy system, and agree on a meeting spot and check-in times.
- Watch your drink and never leave it unattended.
- Use official taxis or rideshares, and confirm the license plate before you get in.
- Check the U.S. State Department's travel advisory for your destination, and sign up for STEP alerts.
