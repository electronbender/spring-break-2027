🌴 Mexico & Caribbean Price Guide

Mexico and the Caribbean cost more upfront, but all-inclusive pricing makes the total predictable: one price covers room, meals and drinks.

What the package price covers

  • All-inclusive resort stay, meals and drinks.
  • Airport transfers, plus flights if you choose an air package.
  • Taxes and fees vary by operator. Ask for the out-the-door price.
  • Party packages are usually a separate add-on.

Known 2027 data points

  • Inertia Tours' Cabo trips ran $899–$1,299 per person all-inclusive without flights in 2025. 2027 prices are TBA.
  • Fox World Travel lists a 7-night all-inclusive Punta Cana trip for Mar 21–28, 2027 starting at $2,791 per person.
  • STS Travel names Cancun, Cabo, Punta Cana and Nassau its top destinations for 2027.

Hidden costs to budget for

  • Tips for resort staff, excursions and boat parties.
  • Departure or tourist taxes, which are sometimes collected on arrival.
  • Travel insurance and a passport (see Passports & Travel Docs).
