Where to go

  • The Strip: A1A along the beach from Las Olas Blvd north to Sunrise Blvd.
  • Las Olas Boulevard: upscale shops and restaurants to the east, newer clubs to the west.
  • Riverwalk along the New River for waterfront bars.

Nightlife

  • Elbo Room: the oldest spring break bar in town, right across from the beach and featured in Where the Boys Are.
  • Rock Bar (Fort Lauderdale Beach Blvd): daily pool parties in March.
  • SW 2nd Street (Himmarshee/Cooley Hammock area): bars and clubs after dark.

Rules and tips

  • Alcohol is prohibited on the beach.
  • Expect a heavy police presence on the beach and downtown, plus curfews for under-18s.
  • Ocean Rescue posts beach-flag conditions daily. Swim near lifeguards.
