Panama City Beach

More than 27 miles of white-sand Gulf beach, moderate prices and big beachfront clubs. MTV made Panama City Beach the default U.S. spring break, and it still draws huge college crowds every March.

Where to stay

  • Holiday Inn Resort Panama City Beach: long known as "Spring Break Central," with a beachside stage and daytime events on the west end.
  • Beachfront condos and hotels along Front Beach Road. Rooms with kitchens go first, so book early.
  • Tour operators block-book hotels and condos, which often include party passes.

Nightlife

  • Harpoon Harry's Beach Club (Front Beach Rd): the main day-and-night venue for sponsored concerts, foam parties and DJ sets.
  • Hammerhead Fred's (Thomas Dr): a classic club known for foam and glow-paint parties.
  • Longboards (Thomas Dr): DJ nights.
  • Sharky's Beach Club and Coyote Ugly Saloon: two of the biggest student bars on the beach.

Days on the beach

  • The Gulf is calm and flat, so think jet skis, parasailing and sailboats rather than surfing.
  • Sponsored brand events pop up on the beach behind the big hotels.
  • St. Andrews State Park and Shell Island are good for a quieter day.

Rules and tips

  • During March, alcohol is banned on the beach and in commercial parking lots, and glass is never allowed on the sand.
  • Late-night alcohol sales are restricted during spring break, and some beach access points have had night closures. Check the city's 2027 rules in February.
  • Party cards such as the Maniac Party Card bundle cover charges and drink specials at partner clubs.
