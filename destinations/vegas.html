Where to stay

  • Center Strip: Planet Hollywood, MGM Grand and other big resorts put you near the clubs.
  • Budget picks: Treasure Island, Excalibur and Luxor.
  • Downtown: the Golden Nugget, the D and the Plaza for cheaper rooms on Fremont Street.

Pools and clubs

  • Weekend dayclubs: Drai's Beach Club, Marquee Dayclub and Wet Republic.
  • Weekday hotel pools are smaller and cheaper.
  • Buy club and pool tickets in advance, because door and weekend prices are higher.

Eat cheap

  • Casino buffets, food courts and In-N-Out keep costs down.
  • Rockhouse and Señor Frog's are famous for oversized drinks.
