Where to stay

  • Hotels and condos line Collins Avenue, many directly on the beach.
  • Timeshare units such as Westgate Miami Beach come with kitchens. Renting from private owners can cost far less than resort rates.
  • Staying in downtown Miami or Brickell puts you close to Ultra at Bayfront Park.

Music and nightlife

  • Miami Music Week (Mar 22–28) fills clubs and hotel pool decks with DJ showcases.
  • Ultra Music Festival (Mar 26–28, Bayfront Park) closes out the week. Calvin Harris headlines the 2027 Phase 1 lineup.
  • Ocean Drive and Washington Avenue are the classic South Beach strips. Most clubs are 21+.

Days on the beach

  • South Beach's lifeguard towers and Lummus Park are the iconic stretch.
  • Walk the Art Deco Historic District and Española Way.
  • Hotel pool parties during Miami Music Week are the daytime highlight.

Rules and tips

  • Miami Beach's "break up with spring break" campaign has brought parking restrictions, DUI checkpoints, bag checks and curfews it can impose if needed.
  • Glass is not allowed on the beach, and open containers are banned in public areas.
  • Budget more than at Gulf Coast beaches: rooms and drinks cost more.
