The two Negrils

  • Seven Mile Beach: white sand, beach bars and bonfires.
  • The West End Cliffs: small inns, cliff jumping and sunsets.

Nightlife

  • Rick's Café: cliff divers and sunset crowds.
  • Margaritaville Negril and beach-bar theme parties.
  • Live reggae nights on the beach.

Days out

  • Cliff jumping from the Pickled Parrot or Rick's.
  • YS Falls day trips, with rope swings over the falls.
  • Sunset cruises along the cliffs, and day sails to Bloody Bay.

Tips

  • Use red-plate licensed taxis and agree on the fare first.
  • Walking the full beach takes about an hour.
