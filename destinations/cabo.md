## Nightlife

- El Squid Roe: a three-story downtown institution with a big sound system, dancing on every level and a decades-long spring break reputation.
- Cabo Wabo Cantina: live music daily, near the marina.
- The Giggling Marlin and Happy Ending Cantina are classic downtown bars.
- Mango Deck on Medano Beach: the place to start the day, with live music, contests and views of the arch.

## Where to stay

- Student packages center on resorts such as RIU Santa Fe and Corazón Cabo on Medano Beach.
- STS Travel, StudentCity and Inertia Tours all sell Cabo trips.

## Days out

- A boat or water taxi to El Arco and Lover's Beach.
- Booze cruises and sunset sails around Land's End.
- Snorkeling at Pelican Rock, and whale watching through early spring.

## Tips

- The Pacific side has strong currents, so swim at Medano Beach.
- Use authorized airport transportation and agree on taxi prices first.
