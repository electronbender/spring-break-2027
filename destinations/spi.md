## Where to stay

- Isla Grand Beach Resort (Padre Blvd): long the event hub, with daytime and evening pool parties.
- Condos are the island's best value. Tour operators such as Inertia Tours block-book condos and rooms.
- Book early and look for kitchens or a microwave and refrigerator.

## Nightlife

- Clayton's Beach Bar (Padre Blvd): the island's biggest concert venue, with rap and EDM headliners each March.
- Louie's Backyard (Laguna Blvd): bayside DJ nights.
- Tequila Frogs: a club overlooking the Laguna Madre.
- About ten nightspots in all, most within a short shuttle ride.

## Days on the island

- Public Gulf beaches with a long-standing party scene.
- Windsurfing, kiteboarding and sailing on the calm Laguna Madre.
- Padre Island National Seashore to the north for wilder beaches.

## Getting around and tips

- Island Metro runs a free shuttle up and down the island during the day.
- Beach Reach volunteers have offered free late-night rides during spring break.
- Texas DPS has urged spring breakers not to cross into Mexico. Check current advisories before any border trip.
- Party cards such as the Maniac VIP Card cover entry to partner events.
