## Where to stay

- Breezes Resort & Spa (Cable Beach): STS Travel's 2027 spring break base.
- Atlantis on Paradise Island: resort, casino and waterpark (day passes available).
- Cable Beach resorts near the casino.

## Nightlife

- Club Waterloo: a huge club with a maze of rooms.
- Aura (Atlantis) and Bond: VIP nights on tour-operator schedules.
- Señor Frog's on the harbor.
- Clubs open late. Go on nights when tour operators schedule events to skip cover and lines.

## Days out

- Blue Lagoon Island: swim with dolphins and snorkel with stingrays.
- Spring break party cruises.
- Historic Nassau: Fort Fincastle, the Queen's Staircase and the Bay Street straw market.

## Getting around

- Daytime jitney buses are cheap. At night, use taxis and agree on the fare first.
- Expect a bridge toll to Paradise Island and 15% service charges on some bills.
- Wear a cover-up away from the beach or pool.
