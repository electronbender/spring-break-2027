Where to stay

  • Hawaiian Inn: past home of daily spring break pool parties.

Nightlife

  • Razzle's, Ocean Deck and 509 Lounge: the classic spring break clubs, accessible with the local VIP Passport.
  • Concerts at the Bandshell at the end of the boardwalk.

Days out

  • Main Street Pier, the boardwalk and the famous drive-on beach.
  • Daytona International Speedway tours and the Motorsports Hall of Fame.
  • Day trips to Orlando's theme parks or Kennedy Space Center (about 65 miles).

Heads up

  • Bike Week overlaps early March and fills hotels. Check the official dates.
