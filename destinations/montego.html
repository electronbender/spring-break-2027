The Hip Strip

  • Gloucester Avenue runs past Doctor's Cave, Cornwall and Aqua Sol beaches, and it's easy to cover on foot.
  • Doctor's Cave Beach has been a famous swimming spot since the early 1900s.
  • Margaritaville (with its water slide) and the Pier 1 waterfront are the classic party spots.

Days out

  • Dunn's River Falls: climb the cascading waterfall near Ocho Rios.
  • Montego Bay Marine Park for snorkeling.
  • River rafting and zipline tours.

Getting around

  • Use red-plate taxis licensed by the Jamaica Tourist Board, and agree on the fare first.
  • Exchange money at banks or cambios rather than the airport.
  • Use the buddy system at night.
