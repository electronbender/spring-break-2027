Highlights

  • Playa del Carmen's Quinta Avenida (Fifth Avenue): pedestrian shopping, bars and beach clubs.
  • Cenotes for swimming and snorkeling in freshwater caves.
  • Tulum's clifftop ruins and beach clubs.
  • The ferry to Cozumel for diving and day trips.

Where to stay

  • Resort-based all-inclusives line the coast between Playa del Carmen and Tulum.
  • STS Travel offers Riviera Maya trips for 2027. Go Blue Tours has run trips to Playa del Carmen.

Tips

  • Use the ADO bus between Cancun, Playa del Carmen and Tulum. It's cheap and reliable.
  • Many cenotes and eco-parks require biodegradable sunscreen.
