## Classic nightlife

- Palladium: the famous cliffside club with panoramic views of Acapulco Bay.
- Baby'O: "the club that started it all."
- El Alebrije, Mandara and Disco Beach (known for foam parties on Condesa Beach).
- Dress codes are enforced: long pants and a collared shirt for men.

## Sights

- La Quebrada cliff divers, who leap about 136 feet into the surf.
- Fuerte de San Diego, a 1600s fort that's now a history museum.
- Condesa Beach is the main beach.

## Before you go

- The U.S. State Department has a high-level advisory for the state of Guerrero. Read the current advisory before planning a trip.
- Use government-certified taxis and agree on the fare first.
- Hurricane Otis (2023) and Hurricane John (2024) damaged much of the city. Confirm that hotels and venues have reopened.
