## Nightlife

- Imagine: a nightclub built inside a natural cave.
- Oro (at Hard Rock): a high-energy resort club.
- Open-bar beach parties and themed club nights through tour operators.

## Days out

- Catamaran and booze cruises with snorkeling stops.
- Saona Island day trips.
- Buggy and ATV tours through the countryside.

## Tips

- A passport valid for your whole stay is required, and the DR's entry form is completed online (e-Ticket).
- Stick to resort or licensed transportation, and keep valuables in the room safe.
