## On the water

- Bridgewater Channel, under the London Bridge, is the main gathering spot for boats.
- Copper Canyon and the sandbar are the party coves, and most are only reachable by boat.
- Boat rentals start around $300+ per day. Windsor Beach in Lake Havasu State Park has rentals.

## Where to stay

- London Bridge Resort: condo rentals right on Bridgewater Channel.
- Hotels and vacation rentals around the channel book up fast in March.

## Nightlife and sights

- Kokomo: the biggest nightclub on the channel.
- The London Bridge itself, rebuilt here in 1971.
- Parker Dam, downstream on the Colorado River.
