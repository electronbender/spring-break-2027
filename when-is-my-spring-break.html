European universities don't have an American-style spring break. UK and Irish schools take a long Easter vacation; most of the continent closes only for Easter weekend. Greece follows Orthodox Easter (May 2, 2027).

Spring break dates FAQ

When is spring break 2027 for most colleges?

Most U.S. colleges break between March 1 and March 26, 2027. The busiest week is the week of Mar 15 (291 of the 777 schools listed here), followed by the week of Mar 8.

How do I find my school's spring break dates?

Search for your school in the list on this page. Because tour company lists sometimes disagree, always confirm with your school's official academic calendar (usually on the registrar's website) before you book flights or pay a deposit.

Why do the sources list different dates?

Tour companies list either the days with no classes (usually Monday to Friday) or the Saturday-to-Saturday travel week around them. We show Go Blue Tours' class-free dates. Where the two lists disagreed, we checked the school's official 2026–27 academic calendar: Go Blue was right for 29 of the 30 schools and STS for one (Manhattan University). Those 30 schools are marked ✓ Official calendar.

Do European and Australian universities have spring break?

Not in the American sense. UK and Irish universities have a 3–6 week Easter vacation, most of continental Europe closes only for the Easter holidays, and Australian universities have a one-week mid-semester break, usually around Easter (Good Friday is March 26, 2027).

When should I book my spring break trip?

Book as soon as your dates are confirmed. The two busiest March weeks sell out first at the most popular hotels and party packages, and flight prices rise as the season approaches.
